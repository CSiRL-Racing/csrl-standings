Pole +2 · Fastest lap +2 · Clean Race Bonus +2. Playoff rounds pay double (R9) and triple (R10).

Season Rules

Each driver picks 1 Gr.3 car for the season. Each driver gets 1 car change per season, and the swapped car is then locked for the rest of the season. Racing Mediums and Hards are the required tires, and both are mandatory in every race. Tire Wear is fixed at 5x and Fuel is fixed at 2x.
